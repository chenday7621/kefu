## Question

Can this eReader become a photo viewer? If so, how do I operate this feature?

## Candidate Silver Answer

Yes. It has a Photo mode for viewing pictures. Use JPEG or BMP images.
1. Add photos by connecting the reader to a computer with the USB data cable and copying files to the removable disk. If using a Micro SD/SDHC card up to 16GB, power the reader off before inserting or removing the card, then turn it back on.
2. From the main menu, select Photo and press M to enter Photo mode. The front control layout shows the M/menu button, left/right navigation keys, Play/Pause button, and zoom in/out key used for photo viewing.<PIC>
3. Press the left/right navigation keys for the previous or next photo.
4. Press Play/Pause to rotate the photo counter-clockwise.
5. Use the zoom in/out key to zoom the photo.
Large files may take longer to load into the photo viewer and to rotate.

```text
### Connect e-book to the computer

Please connect this device to your computer via USB data cable. This reader will auto turn on and the screen will show "USB Connection", and your computer will identify this reader as a Removable Disk after the connection is completed. Your computer will show 2 Removable disks for this device: One is for built-in internal memory of this device and the other is for Micro SD card (not included) if you have inserted one.

### Manage files

After the device is properly connected to your computer, you can select the removable disk in the "My Computer" and start transfer, copy or delete file in between this device and your computer.

### Inserting a Micro SD card(Not included)

You can expand the memory of this device(up to 16GB) by inserting a micro SD or SDHC card(its also call TF card). First power off the device then insert a micro SD card and turn the device back on.

### Note about the use of Micro SD card

If your device encounters a problem identifying your inserted Micro SD card, turn off the device, pull the memory card out, and reinsert it.

**VERY IMPORTANT:** Make sure to turn the power off when removing or inserting the memory card. Also, do not reset the unit when your memory card is inserted into the device.
```
