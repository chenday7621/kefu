## Question

In the boat's steering position, what does the factory reset screen show?

## Candidate Silver Answer

On the helm multi-function display, the factory reset screen shows the "Factory Reset" title and asks "Reset all parameters to factory setting?" with "Reset" and "Back" buttons.<PIC>
After "Reset" is tapped, the confirmation asks "Are you sure?" with "YES" to reset the settings and "NO" to return without resetting.<PIC>

## Candidate Required Facts

1. **TIP:** To return to the factory reset screen without resetting the settings, tap the “NO” button.
   Source: `E2`; machine proposed.
2. **To reset the settings:** (1) Tap the “Reset” button.
   Source: `E2`; machine proposed.
3. Multi-function display unit (page 36)
   Source: `E1`; machine proposed.
4. This screen can be used to reset the settings to their factory default settings.
   Source: `E2`; machine proposed.
