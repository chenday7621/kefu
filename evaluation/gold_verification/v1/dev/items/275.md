## Question

What is the trip screen shown?

## Candidate Silver Answer

The trip screen is a multi-function display page for engine-operation hours, fuel-consumption, and trip data. The shown screen has a menu, scrollbar, and Reset button; its visible four menu entries are water temperature, average fuel economy, trip distance, and fuel used.<PIC> The menu shows four items at a time; scroll with the scrollbar. Touch and hold an item to reset it, or tap Reset to reset all resettable items; asterisked items cannot be reset.
