## Question

If I want to listen to music on my phone, how do I turn on the sound system?

## Candidate Silver Answer

The boat manual does not give the stereo receiver's exact power-button or Bluetooth pairing sequence; it says to refer to the separate stereo system Operation Manual included with the boat. From this manual, first make sure the boat's accessory power is available: in normal operation keep the "START" and "HOUSE" battery switches in the ON position and leave "EMERG PARALLEL" OFF, because the house battery powers the audio system.
Operate the sound system from the stereo receiver at the helm.<PIC>
For phone music, use one of the supported stereo inputs: Bluetooth, the auxiliary input jack, or the USB terminal. The auxiliary input jack and USB terminal are the two labeled inputs shown together.<PIC>

## Battery switches

The batteries and switch assembly are located in the battery compartment. This boat uses two marine batteries. One battery is designated as the start battery. The start battery is connected to the starter circuit on the engine to provide current for the starter motor. The other battery is designated as the house battery. The house battery provides power for the various accessory circuits on the boat, such as the circuits for the lighting, bilge pump, blowers, and audio system.

1. Battery switch assembly
2. Start battery
3. House battery

1. “HOUSE” switch (red)
2. “EMERG PARALLEL” switch (yellow)
3. “START” switch (red)

![Illustration](插图/Manual09_43.jpg)

1. ON position (green)
2. OFF position (red)

![Illustration](插图/Manual09_44.jpg)

In normal operation, keep the “START” and “HOUSE” switches in the ON position. Keep the “EMERG PARALLEL” switch in the OFF position. This setting allows both batteries to be charged automatically while the engine is running. **NOTICE:** Do not turn the “HOUSE” switch to the OFF position if the boat is moored in the water as it will prevent the anchor light from functioning. If the start battery is discharged, turn the “EMERG PARALLEL” switch to the ON position to start the engine. Once the engine is started or the start battery is charged, turn the “EMERG PARALLEL” switch to the OFF position.
