## Question

How do I load the boat when the cruise is over?

## Candidate Silver Answer

1. Disconnect the trailer lights from the towing vehicle.
2. Back the trailer down the ramp as close to 90 degrees to the shoreline as possible. If possible, have a second person stand to the side as an observer. Stop when the tops of the trailer fenders are about 3 inches above the waterline.
3. With the boat moving at the slowest idle speed, guide it onto the support rails. Use throttle only if needed for steering; too much throttle can make the boat jump over the front of the trailer and injure the operator or bystanders.
4. Make sure the boat is centered on the support rails and headed straight for the bow stop. Ease forward until the bow rests against the bow stop. The winch line is not designed to pull the boat onto the trailer.
5. Attach and tighten the winch line to the bow eye, and also use the chain to secure the bow; the winch line alone is not enough for trailering.<PIC>
6. Pull the trailer up the ramp out of other boaters' way. Attach the bow and stern tie-downs, using the stern eyes for the stern tie-downs, then reconnect the trailer lights.<PIC>
7. Perform the post-operation checks.

## Loading

(1) Disconnect the trailer lights from the towing vehicle. (2) Back the trailer down the ramp as close to 90 to the shoreline as you can. If possible, have a second person act as an observer while standing to the side of the trailer. Stop when the tops of the trailer’s fenders are about 3 inches above the waterline. (3) With the boat moving at the slowest idle speed, guide the boat onto the support rails. Use throttle only if necessary for steering ability. WARNING! Using too much throttle can cause the boat to jump over the front of the trailer, which can result in injury to the boat operator and bystanders. (4) Make sure the boat is centered on the support rails and is headed straight for the bow stop (bumper board). Ease the boat forward until the bow rests against the bow stop. **NOTICE:** The winch line is not designed to pull the boat onto the trailer. (5) Attach and tighten the winch line. **NOTICE:** The winch line should not be the only line securing the bow during trailering. Use the chain along with the winch to secure the boat to the trailer.
